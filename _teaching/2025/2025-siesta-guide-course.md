This page provides the materials for the course **"Siesta: A Complete Guide"**. The course offers a comprehensive introduction to electronic structure calculations using the **SIESTA** code, bridging the gap between fundamental Density Functional Theory (DFT) concepts and practical simulations.

You can access the input files, examples, and exercises used in this course on the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/bastoscmo/siesta-complete-guide).

## Course Overview

The material covers a wide range of topics, from basic installation to advanced properties characterization:

* **Fundamentals:** Introduction to DFT, Hohenberg-Kohn theorems, Kohn-Sham formalism, and Pseudopotentials.
* **Getting Started with SIESTA:** Installation, FDF format, and basic simulation parameters.
* **Molecular & Solid State Physics:** Structural optimization, Crystal structure, Band theory, and Density of States (DOS).
* **Advanced Features:** Ab Initio Molecular Dynamics, van der Waals corrections (Grimme), Phonons, among others.
