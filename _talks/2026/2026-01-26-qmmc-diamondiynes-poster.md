This poster was presented at **QMMC 2026**, the Advanced School on Quantum Modelling of Materials with CRYSTAL, held at the Federal Fluminense University (UFF), Aterrado Campus, in Volta Redonda.

The poster presented the diamondiynes family of three-dimensional carbon allotropes, based on the paper "[Entangled Interlocked Diamond-like (Diamondiynes) Lattices](/publication/2025-09-25-diamondiynes-lattices)" (ACS Omega, 2025).

### Main topics covered:
* **A New Class of Carbon Allotropes:** Diamondiynes — crystalline, interlocked sp/sp³ carbon sublattices capable of relative movement without covalent bonds between them, the first structures of their kind to be reported.
* **Computational Methodology:** DFT calculations (SIESTA, PBE functional with a DZP basis set; HSE06 hybrid functional for accurate band-gap prediction) with Grimme's DFT-D3 dispersion correction, complemented by *ab initio* molecular dynamics (DFTB+, 300 K and 1000 K) to assess thermal stability.
* **Stability and Mechanical Properties:** Thermodynamic stability confirmed by a cohesive energy of approximately -6.7 eV/atom and no phase transition or degradation up to 1000 K; high flexibility, with strain at failure around 25% and a tunable Young's modulus ranging from 19 to 137 GPa.
* **Electronic Properties:** Wide electronic band gaps ranging from 2.2 to 4.0 eV.
