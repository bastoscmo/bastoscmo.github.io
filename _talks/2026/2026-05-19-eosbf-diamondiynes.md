This talk was presented at **EOSBF 2026**, the Brazilian Physical Society's Autumn Meeting, held in Cuiabá, Mato Grosso.

The presentation introduced the diamondiynes family of three-dimensional carbon allotropes, based on the paper "[Entangled Interlocked Diamond-like (Diamondiynes) Lattices](/publication/2025-09-25-diamondiynes-lattices)" (ACS Omega, 2025).

### Main topics covered:
* **A New Class of Carbon Allotropes:** Diamondiynes — 3D structures formed by folding and interconnecting linear sp/sp³ carbon chains into entangled, interlocked sublattices (unfolded, two-fold symmetric/unsymmetric, three-fold, and four-fold configurations).
* **Computational Methodology:** First-principles DFT (SIESTA, with DFT+D3 van der Waals corrections) combined with *ab initio* molecular dynamics to assess thermodynamic stability at 300 K and 1000 K.
* **Thermodynamic Stability and Mobility:** All five diamondiyne structures remain stable at both temperatures; the interlocked sublattices show high relative mobility while individual C-C bonds stay intact, giving rise to a movable, interlocked lattice architecture.
* **Electronic and Mechanical Properties:** Wide-band-gap semiconductors (2.2-4.0 eV) with promise for optoelectronic applications, combined with a mechanical response that is both highly resistant and flexible compared to conventional diamond-like structures.
