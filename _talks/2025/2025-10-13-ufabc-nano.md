This colloquium was presented at the **Federal University of ABC (UFABC)** as part of the Nanomaterials Graduate Program seminar series.

In this talk, I discussed the role of computational simulations in predicting the physical properties of novel materials, from theoretical foundations to practical applications in material discovery.

### Main topics covered:
* **The Rise of 2D Materials:** Overview of graphene and the expanding family of two-dimensional systems.
* **Theoretical Framework:** Approaches to solving the many-body Schrödinger equation and predicting material properties.
* **Novel Systems:**
    * Stability and properties of Monolayer Noble Metals.
    * Exploration of Entangled Carbon Allotropes.
    * Recent results on ITO@PMMA nanocomposites and Sn3O4 nanowires.
