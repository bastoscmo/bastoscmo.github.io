This presentation was delivered at the **XXIII B-MRS Meeting** (Brazilian Materials Research Society) in Salvador, Bahia.

In this talk, I presented an innovative cost-effective protocol for predicting and characterizing the optoelectronic properties of $Mo_{1-x}W_{x}Se_{2}$ alloys. The work investigates the thermodynamic stability at the atomistic level and focuses on the impact of excitonic effects using a data-driven workflow.

### Key Topics Covered:
* Engineering 2D Materials and Transition Metal Dichalcogenides (TMDs).
* Thermodynamic stability of MoWSe2 alloys.
* Prediction of optical properties dominated by strongly bound electron-hole pairs (excitons).
* Application of First-Principles Statistical Investigation.
