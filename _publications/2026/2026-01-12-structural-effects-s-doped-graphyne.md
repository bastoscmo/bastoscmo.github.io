The graphyne family represents a versatile class of carbon-based materials with promising potential for technological applications. In this work, we analyze the influence of structural changes on the mechanical, electronic, and optical properties of sulfur-doped graphyne (S-GY).

**Key Findings:**
* **Electronic Structure**: The S-GY monolayer is an indirect band-gap semiconductor with a gap of approximately 1.3 eV.
* **Stability**: Thermodynamic stability was confirmed through phonon dispersion and *ab initio* molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations.
* **Optoelectronic Properties**: The material shows a maximum power-conversion efficiency of ~22%, making it a candidate for next-generation solar technologies.

**Methodology:**
The study used first-principles calculations based on Density Functional Theory (DFT) as implemented in the VASP package, with PBE and HSE06 functionals.
