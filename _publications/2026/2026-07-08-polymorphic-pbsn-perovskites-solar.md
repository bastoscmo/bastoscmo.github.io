This study combines first-principles calculations with statistical thermodynamic modeling (based on the generalized quasichemical approximation) to investigate mixed Pb/Sn halide perovskite alloys as candidates for next-generation solar-harvesting devices.

**Key Findings:**
* **Polymorphic and Compositional Effects**: Power conversion efficiency at operating temperature exhibits maximum performance for tin content above roughly 70%.
* **Optoelectronic Performance**: The bromide-based variant achieves power conversion efficiencies of approximately 29%.
* **Thermodynamic Stability**: The bromide-based alloy displays a notably low critical ordering temperature, around 28 K, indicating favorable compositional stability across a wide temperature range.

**Methodology:**
The study combined first-principles density functional theory with a statistical approach based on the generalized quasichemical approximation to evaluate the polymorphic, thermodynamic, and optoelectronic properties of the mixed-halide alloy as a function of composition.
