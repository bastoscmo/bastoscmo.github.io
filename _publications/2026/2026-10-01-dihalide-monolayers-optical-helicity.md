This work investigates the excitonic and optical properties of 2H-phase group-IV transition-metal dihalide monolayers, examining the interplay between strong excitonic effects and optical helicity in the context of solar-energy-harvesting applications.

**Methodology:**
The electronic, optical, and excitonic properties of the monolayers were evaluated using a first-principles framework combining density functional theory, maximally localized Wannier-function-based tight binding, and the Bethe-Salpeter equation — the same computational pipeline (WanTiBEXOS) used across this group's related work on 2D dichalcogenide and dihalide monolayers.
