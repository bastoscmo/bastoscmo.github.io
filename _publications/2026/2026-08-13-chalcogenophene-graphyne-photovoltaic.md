Chalcogenophene-modified graphyne (chp-GY) monolayers constitute a versatile family of two-dimensional semiconductors with highly tunable physical properties. This work investigates the structural stability and optoelectronic response of chp-GY monolayers through first-principles calculations, assessing their potential for photovoltaic applications.

**Methodology:**
The structural, electronic, and optical properties of the chalcogenophene-modified graphyne monolayers were evaluated using first-principles density functional theory.
